## Design Context

This context applies to the requested benchmark HTML reports, not to a redesign of the native Arion application.

### Users

The product owner and engineering reviewers compare rewrite iterations, inspect failures and decide whether evidence supports macOS release readiness.

### Brand Personality

Precise, candid and composed. Confidence comes from inspectable evidence and clearly stated limitations.

### Aesthetic Direction

User-confirmed light experiment-report style. Use restrained editorial hierarchy, readable source/output comparisons, searchable failures and progressive disclosure of raw evidence. Avoid decorative dashboards or unqualified success scores.

### Design Principles

- Put the decision, comparison population and evidence scope together.
- Keep raw, validated and delivered output distinct.
- Display all cases, including failures and unchanged results.
- Never imply cross-round percentages are paired when corpora differ.
- Make release gates and missing evidence visible alongside improvements.
