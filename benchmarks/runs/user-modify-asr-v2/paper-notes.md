# Primary-paper mechanisms and local adaptations

- Qwen3-ASR technical report, section 2.2: ASR-only post-training suppresses general instruction following while learning background-context use. Pair arrows and an arbitrary slot wrapper are not a documented replacement interface. Local v1 outputs show wrong-form competition, but no attention attribution was measured.
- Gong et al., Interspeech 2024, sections 3.1/5.1: prompts and special bias tags are trained with LoRA, empty-context examples and negative contexts. Larger lists degrade this SpeechLLM setup. Its exact list-size threshold and trained-tag results do not transfer to untuned Qwen3-ASR-1.7B.
- RECAST, Findings EMNLP 2025: contrastively trains decoder-state/keyword retrieval and uses a ranked shortlist for downstream audio recognition. Current experiment takes the compact-list principle, not its trained embedding retriever or published accuracy.
- Kong et al., arXiv:2512.21828v1, sections 2.2/4.2: retrieves compact hotword sets, removes already well-recognized words, and combines retrieval with supervised/RL adaptation. More retrieved words do not monotonically improve final recognition. Here we skip already recognized targets and compare at most two candidates; no GLCLAP training or GRPO was performed.
- Harding et al., Interspeech 2023, section 2.1.6: selectively activates trained adapters to reduce over-biasing. The local lexical/phonetic routing rule is a simpler engineering adaptation and cannot inherit the paper's guarantees.
- Huber and Waibel, arXiv:2608.05759v1: evaluates Qwen3-ASR and other speech LLMs with distractor lists and filtered contexts, identifying practical filtering cost and model/scale differences. Supports separate term, ordinary-word and latency reporting, not a claim that our small-list result reproduces its system.

Local optimization uses prior wrong forms only as text-derived phonetic retrieval keys. Only correct destination spellings enter ASR context. The same original audio is decoded again for actual recognition; no output string substitution occurs. This cannot recover alternatives omitted by both the first pass and the approved aliases. Raw spelling error rate is supplemented with entity hit and unrelated memory-entity insertion proxies.
